# Differences Across the 40 Agreement Texts (AGR-2026-001 to AGR-2026-040)

1. **Clause Numbering, Openings, and Ordering**: Entries vary between Section 1.01., ARTICLE I., numbered headings like 1.1 Definitions, or starting directly with covenants (such as Section 6.15. or Section 8.01.) before definitions.
2. **Borrower Terminology & Title Mismatches**: Borrowers appear under "Borrower" means ... or "Company" means .... In AGR-2026-016, greement_title lists "Harlow Industrial Supply, Inc.", whereas greement_text defines "Borrower" means Harlow Industrial Supply Corporation, a Delaware corporation.
3. **Margin Structures & Later Amendments**: Some agreements specify a flat rate/margin (in ps, asis points per annum, or %, sometimes split between Term SOFR and Base Rate), while others use leverage pricing grids (Level, Tier, Category, or leverage bands). In AGR-2026-019, Section 2.03 Amendment replaces Level III (>= 3.00:1.00) from 175 bps to 200 bps effective on the Restatement Date, requiring full-text reading rather than stopping at the first rate.
4. **Commitment Amounts**: Amounts appear as comma-separated numbers ($500,000,000), decimal millions ($250.0 million), or spelled-out text paired with figures (Seven Hundred Fifty Million Dollars (,000,000)), occasionally alongside availability windows.
5. **Maturity Dates**: Dates appear as explicit calendar dates (e.g., July 7, 2031) or relative terms from the Closing Date (e.g., "the fifth anniversary of the Closing Date" or "the seventh anniversary of the Closing Date"), requiring calculation and YYYY-MM-DD normalization.
6. **Covenant Presence**: Leverage and interest coverage covenants appear with varying section labels and phrasing, or may be omitted (None), requiring careful extraction and verbatim source quotes.
