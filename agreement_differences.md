# Differences Across the 40 Agreement Texts (AGR-2026-001 to AGR-2026-040)

1. **Clause Numbering, Openings, and Ordering**: Texts vary between Section 1.01., ARTICLE I., numbered subsections like 1.1 Definitions, or starting directly with financial covenants (Section 6.15. The Borrower shall not permit... or Section 8.01. ...) before definitions.
2. **Borrower Terminology & Title Discrepancies**: Borrowers are introduced as "Borrower" means ... or "Company" means .... In AGR-2026-016, greement_title shows "Harlow Industrial Supply, Inc.", whereas greement_text defines "Borrower" means Harlow Industrial Supply Corporation, a Delaware corporation.
3. **Margin Formats & Mid-Text Amendments**: Some agreements specify a flat rate/margin (in ps, asis points per annum, or %, sometimes split across Term SOFR and Base Rate), while others use leverage pricing grids (Level, Tier, Category, or ratio ranges). In AGR-2026-019, Section 2.03 Amendment replaces Level III (>= 3.00:1.00) from 175 bps to 200 bps effective on the Restatement Date, requiring full-text reading rather than stopping at the first rate.
4. **Commitment Amounts**: Amounts appear as numeric figures with commas ($500,000,000), decimal millions ($250.0 million), or spelled-out words paired with numbers (Seven Hundred Fifty Million Dollars (,000,000)), and sometimes include availability windows (such as 18 or 24 months for delayed draw facilities).
5. **Maturity Dates**: Some agreements give an explicit calendar date (e.g., July 7, 2031), while others state a relative duration from the Closing Date (e.g., "the fifth anniversary of the Closing Date" or "the seventh anniversary of the Closing Date"), requiring date calculation normalized to YYYY-MM-DD.
6. **Covenant Presence**: Leverage and interest coverage covenants appear across entries with varying section numbers and wording, and must be extracted accurately or left None when absent.
